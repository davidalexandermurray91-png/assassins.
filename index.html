ASSASSINS

Goal

Survive nights in a huge hotel. Earn money by day, buy gear, fight, ride or hide at night.

Combat

Left-click / ATK: Sword melee or Gun raycast.

Riding

SPACE to jump. Land on an assassin to ride it. While you're on its back it CANNOT hit you, and every ATK does bonus damage — even bare-handed. SPACE again to jump off.

Night

H near a wardrobe or bed: hide 8s (10s cooldown). Shift: sprint (alerts assassins!).

Day

E: work (+$10). C: buy Sword / Gun / Purple Cloak. R near bed: sleep (restore 1 HP).

Doctor

Find the Doctor in the lobby. E gives +1 extra life (up to 10), then he needs 25s to prepare another.

PLGs

Green-eyed golems that defend you. Tough but not invincible — assassins can bring them down.

Semi-Players

Purple half-players. Untamed, they attack assassins AND you. Buy the Purple Cloak, then press E next to one to tame it. Tamed ones fight for you and stay with you through the day.

Floors

Walk onto stairs (S) to switch between Floor 1 and the Basement.

Account

Press ESC (or the ACCOUNT button) on the title screen to log in or create an account. Progress saves automatically.
